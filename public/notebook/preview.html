Perception & robot control

IrisIRIS

Intelligent Robotic Instrument Sorter

NUS CDE3301 · with SNEC · ROS 2, C++, MoveIt, RealSense, YOLO, DINO, React

The problem

After eye surgery, staff sort delicate instruments by hand. IRIS looks at a cluttered tray, works out which instrument sits on top, and moves it to its slot only after an operator approves the pick.

What I built

  • 1Detection and tracking of ophthalmic instruments from RGB-D, with DINO identity checks when a set needs them
  • 2Topmost-first pick order from a contact graph between overlapping instruments
  • 3xArm motion through MoveIt, shared workspace limits and explicit operator approval
